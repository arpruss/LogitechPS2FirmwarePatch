Clock: 16mhz

P1.0: read digital button row (mask 0x20 at payload[0x11]?)
P1.1: LED, active low
P1.2: read digital button row
P0.0: write
P0.2: 
P0.3-5: select matrix column

P0.1: little vibration motor, independent of PWM
P0.2: big vibration motor, uses PWM
P0.7: vibration motor PWM

When P0.4 set, P1.0=vibration button, P1.2=0
     P0.5 set, P1.0=1, P1.2=1
